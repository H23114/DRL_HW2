Stability analysis summary
==========================

More stable method near the end of training: SARSA

How stability is measured:
- Mean reward across repeated runs reflects typical learning progress.
- Rolling reward standard deviation reflects how strongly rewards fluctuate during training.

Q-learning final mean reward: -77.200
Q-learning final rolling std: 48.363
SARSA final mean reward: -27.500
SARSA final rolling std: 14.663

Interpretation:
SARSA usually reflects exploration more directly because its update target uses the actual next epsilon-greedy action. If exploration might step near the cliff and incur large penalties, SARSA learns to account for that risk during training, so it often adopts a more conservative route. Q-learning updates toward the greedy max next action instead, so it can look more optimistic about risky states and may prefer a shorter path closer to the cliff.
